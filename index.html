Common questions

What is the minimum order?

Usually one 20 ft container. For a first order we can discuss smaller trial lots.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes. Send an enquiry with your address and we will courier a sample of your chosen variety.

Do you offer private label packing?

Yes. We pack in 5 kg to 50 kg bags with your brand, artwork and barcode.

Which ports do you ship from?

Mainly Mundra and Nhava Sheva, and we can arrange other Indian ports on request.
